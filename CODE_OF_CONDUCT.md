# Community conduct

NeuroImage welcomes contributions from learners, educators, clinicians, researchers and software developers. Treat people with respect, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and acknowledge the work of original data and software authors.

Use considerate language. Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted personal attention and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable in project discussions and events. Keep reports and examples free of personal or patient information.

Contact the maintainer, Shanmu Jin, at jinshanmu1129@pku.edu.cn to report a concern privately. Describe what happened and where it occurred. Reports will be handled with discretion; information may be shared when needed to investigate or protect participants. The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, limit participation or exclude an account after considering the conduct and context. A person involved in a complaint should not decide that complaint's outcome.

This policy applies to project repositories, issue discussions, contribution reviews and spaces where participants represent the project. Respectful criticism of software, evidence and anatomical interpretations is welcome.
