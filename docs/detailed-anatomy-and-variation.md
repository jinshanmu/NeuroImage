## Normal variation observations

Ten original healthy-volunteer THU MRA cases are currently included: 001, 006, 007, 009, 010, 014, 015, 019, 021 and 030. Cases 015 and 019 add complete original twenty-label inventories with their source landmarks inside the delivered image. The original eight teaching cases and their archived curricula retain their source bindings. Other cohort definitions remain archived rather than enabled; every dataset family is limited to ten independent cases.

The complete 109-case inventory and historical verification receipts remain source evidence. The selected ten cases retain complete original inputs and unchanged package bindings without requiring the removed mixed-cohort ZIPs; the original archive hashes and acquisition definitions remain documented.

The following course counts describe preserved testing-branch records, including now-excluded statistical/image sources; they do not describe current application entries. The **Normal arterial variation: source observations across eight volunteers** course contains 16 views: eight anterior-region views followed by eight posterior-region views. Each view opens its own case and exact package. The **Interpreting normal variation across individual and population sources** course contains 11 views separating individual MRA observations, arterial occurrence, venous field dispersion, cerebellar assignment and observer support.

In an MRA source, open **Learn → Observation and interpretation** for its label inventory and comparison guide. Record the visible signal, labelled extent, source side and unresolved interpretation separately. Missing categorical labels do not establish anatomical absence, hypoplasia or a fetal-type configuration. The cases were selected to span source annotation patterns, not to estimate prevalence. No newly added case is presented as an independently adjudicated named variant.

## Validation and source limits

Original MRA and CT sample equality, physical coordinates, retained surface geometry, exact lesson targets and browser recovery are engineering checks. They do not replace independent anatomical review. Per-source evidence is recorded in the dated configuration receipts; acquired data and rendered teaching captures stay outside software distributions.

Remaining anatomical work is explicit and separate from these complete source modules:

- Expert review of named individual vascular variants against original images and source landmarks.
- Source-supported cisternal membranes and fine arachnoid boundaries; current observation anchors do not define membranes.
- Further verified foraminal, dural and cranial-nerve segment labels where the upstream source supplies separate definitions.
- Finer thalamic, hypothalamic, cortical and cerebellar subdivisions when their source naming and geometry can be established.
- Additional normal individual examples and acquisition contrasts, followed by anatomical and teaching evaluation.

No anatomy-completeness percentage is inferred from the number of source entries. Existing source models, measured images, categorical labels, overlapping regions and population fields retain their distinct meanings.
